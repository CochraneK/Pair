# PAIR ethics determination request — draft

**Status:** draft for institutional submission; no ethics status is implied by this document.

## Study
PAIR evaluates responses of publicly available consumer AI chat products and fixed API model configurations to **fictional, researcher-generated psychosis-related prompts**.

## Data sources
The planned benchmark itself will use:
- synthetic fictional prompts;
- AI-generated responses;
- product/API execution metadata;
- ratings produced by research-team clinicians as part of their research duties.

The benchmark does **not** intend to recruit patients, students, or members of the public as research participants and does not intend to ingest identifiable clinical records.

## Main activities
1. submit frozen fictional prompts to consumer chat products and/or APIs;
2. preserve model responses and technical metadata;
3. have clinician raters evaluate outputs using a predefined rubric;
4. analyse response appropriateness, failure modes, stability and clinical response-policy calibration.

## Potentially relevant institutional question
Please determine whether this project is:
- not human-subject research;
- exempt human-subject research;
- or requires another review pathway under local institutional policy.

The research team will use the institution's determination rather than self-designating the project as exempt.

## Privacy
No patient identifiers are planned. Research product accounts are separated from personal accounts. Credentials, account identifiers and private screenshots are excluded from the public repository.

## Risk
The main foreseeable risks concern:
- accidental public release of account/session information;
- overinterpretation of model behavior as clinical capability;
- exposure of research staff to distressing psychiatric content.

Mitigations include access separation, metadata minimization, public/private data boundaries and bounded interpretation rules.

## Public materials
The public GitHub repository contains synthetic research materials, methods, schemas and code. Raw credentials and restricted/private information are excluded.

## Clinical ratings
Clinician ratings are research annotations, not patient-care decisions. If the institution regards staff raters as research participants for local-policy purposes, the team requests guidance on the appropriate consent/documentation pathway.

## Requested determination record
Please provide a dated written determination that can be stored with the study records and accurately described in manuscripts and preregistration materials.
