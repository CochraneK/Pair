# PAIR-C companion paper plan

PAIR-C should remain a separate benchmark/methodology manuscript rather than be merged into the A+B consumer-product paper. Its proposed signature is: psychosis-specific one-clinical-cue-at-a-time minimal contrasts, clinician-defined acceptable response-policy intervals, symmetric under-response/over-response scoring, and a separate nuisance-invariance module. Broad counterfactual mental-health auditing is not novel after SIM-VAIL; generic clinical calibration is also occupied by Safe-Psych and K-Bench. The C manuscript should therefore be written only after real clinician interval annotations and fixed-model API results exist.

Suggested title: **Calibrating Conversational Intervention Boundaries for Psychosis-Related Disclosures in Large Language Models: The PAIR-C Benchmark**.

Primary metrics: CCA, URS, ORS, relevant sensitivity/directional concordance, and nuisance invariance (only after a genuine nuisance set is frozen).
