# REAL_DATA_SWAP_MAP

Before submission, resolve every `[[...]]` placeholder in the formatted master manuscript.

## Mandatory real-world replacements
1. Ethics determination: institution, classification, reference ID, date.
2. Preregistration: platform, identifier, date, link.
3. Product metadata: exact product/model label, surface, subscription, date/time, settings.
4. Clinical validation: real reviewer IDs/pseudonyms, manipulation success, final `[L,U]`, adjudication rule.
5. Execution flow: eligible outputs, failures, retries, quota/fallback.
6. Aim 1: CLMM estimates, CI, model probabilities, reliability, stability.
7. Aim 2: CCA, URS, ORS, directional concordance, clustered uncertainty.
8. Persistent data/code archive DOI.
9. Author, funding, COI, acknowledgements, CRediT, AI-use statements.
10. Refresh references immediately before submission.

Synthetic rehearsal files must never be merged into the empirical Results dataset.
