# Reviewer #2 stress test — PAIR A+B

**Simulated venue:** npj Digital Medicine  
**Pre-data decision:** major-revision risk reduced; manuscript is not submission-ready until real gates are filled.

## Major issues found and action taken
1. **Blinding claim corrected.** Appropriateness raters must see the prompt-response pair, so they cannot meaningfully be blinded to psychosis/control condition. The defensible blind is product identity only.
2. **P5 matching risk addressed.** Disorganized communication changes linguistic organization more extensively than P1–P4. A prespecified P1–P4-only sensitivity analysis is now required.
3. **Product sampling frame made explicit.** Product inclusion/exclusion must be frozen before outcomes and major eligible products not tested must be disclosed.
4. **Version-break rule added.** If product model/mode/routing materially changes during collection, pause or create a new stratum; never pool silently.
5. **Brand-leakage audit added.** Product self-identification, proprietary citation style, formatting, or safety-language signatures may compromise blinding; unresolved leakage is reported rather than semantically edited away.
6. **Sample-size interpretation narrowed.** The design is strongest for the overall psychosis-vs-control effect. Product interactions/domain contrasts remain secondary. Simulation-based pre-data precision checks are required.
7. **Primary mixed-model parameterization remains a pre-analysis gate.** Random effects, convergence and proportional-odds diagnostics must be frozen before empirical ratings are inspected.
8. **Aim 2 remains exploratory.** Six scenario families are the effective clinical replication units; repeated generations cannot inflate N.

## Remaining submission blockers
- real clinician item validation and rating reliability;
- real ethics determination and preregistration status;
- exact product/configuration manifests;
- frozen statistical model/sessionInfo;
- Data/Code Availability;
- empirical Results/Discussion only.

## Reviewer-style bottom line
If the real collection is clean and the primary effect is estimated with useful precision, A+B is a credible product-audit/digital-medicine paper. Its novelty is moderate; methodological cleanliness and disciplined claim scope will determine publishability.
