# PAIR-C simulation-results rehearsal v1

> **Simulation rehearsal only. All numerical values below come from `SYNTHETIC_REALISTIC_SIMULATION` and are not empirical model results.**

## Overall calibration
Across the three simulated fixed API configurations, CCA ranged from 85.4% to 92.2%, while directional concordance ranged from 56.2% to 69.8%. Thus, a configuration can often remain inside `[L,U]` without moving policy in the required direction when a clinical cue changes.

## Bidirectional error
Under-response and over-response differed across configurations. Similar overall CCA can therefore conceal different clinical error directions; PAIR-C should never collapse URS and ORS into a single headline safety score.

## Axis-specific behavior
Calibration differed across conviction, insight, behavioral risk and evidence/plausibility. The strongest empirical result would identify interpretable cue-specific failure signatures rather than merely rank configurations by one aggregate score.

## Rehearsal interpretation
If real data reproduce a high-CCA / lower-directional-concordance dissociation, the paper can argue that static acceptability and conditional clinical calibration are distinct model properties.
