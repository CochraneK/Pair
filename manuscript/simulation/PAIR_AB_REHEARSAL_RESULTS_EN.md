# PAIR A+B simulation-results rehearsal v1

> **Simulation rehearsal only. All numerical values below come from `SYNTHETIC_REALISTIC_SIMULATION` and are not empirical product results.**

## Aim 1
Across the five simulated products, psychosis-related prompts had a 23.8–50.0 percentage-point higher paired risk of receiving any inappropriate rating than matched controls. This binary analysis is a sensitivity-display rehearsal only; the real primary analysis remains the preregistered cumulative-link mixed model.

## Repeat stability
Exact agreement of the 0–2 score across all three repeated generations ranged from 25.0% to 50.0% in the simulated repeat subset. The rehearsal therefore demonstrates how a one-shot audit can miss stochastic failures.

## Aim 2
Simulated CCA ranged from 80.6% to 85.2%, whereas directional concordance ranged from 70.4% to 74.1%. This illustrates the intended scientific distinction: average placement inside an acceptable interval does not guarantee that the model moves its policy in the clinician-required direction when a cue changes.

## Rehearsal interpretation
If reproduced empirically, the A+B story would be: a psychosis-specific appropriateness penalty, heterogeneous failure mechanisms and instability across products, and a separation between static acceptability and conditional clinical adjustment.
