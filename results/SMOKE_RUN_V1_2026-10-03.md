## Design bug found and corrected
During the smoke run, the engineering fixture incorrectly treated Plan C `evidence/plausibility` as if it were a nuisance-invariance axis.

That is inconsistent with the benchmark design. `evidence/plausibility` is a **clinically relevant axis** and may require a policy shift in the opposite direction from conviction/risk—for example, stronger externally verifiable evidence may reduce inappropriate psychiatric escalation or over-pathologisation.

Correction:
- `evidence/plausibility` remains a relevant-sensitivity axis;
- its expected direction is derived from the frozen clinician/assumed `[L,U]` intervals, not hard-coded as positive;
- **nuisance invariance is assessed only on dedicated clinically irrelevant contrast pairs**, not on the evidence/plausibility axis;
- the 64-item C pilot currently contains the 4 relevant axes only, so NI is not estimable from this 64-item pilot unless a nuisance subset is added as a separate frozen module.
