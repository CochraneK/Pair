`*` Plan C's ceiling remains high only if its signature is narrow enough. A 2026 Nature Medicine study (SIM-VAIL; DOI 10.1038/s41591-026-04577-2) already includes clinically validated multi-turn mental-health auditing and counterfactual message interventions. Therefore Plan C must not claim generic counterfactual auditing as novelty.

## Decision

### Choose A when
- the team prioritizes a clean, fast first paper;
- the full original prompt set is already close to frozen;
- contributor boundaries should remain maximally simple.

### Choose B when
- the team wants a stronger scientific story without sacrificing the original paper;
- clinicians can provide limited additional ground truth;
- a small pilot is acceptable before full collection.

### Choose C when
- the team explicitly agrees this is a new research question;
- authorship/contribution boundaries are renegotiated;
- a formal novelty search and clinician-ground-truth exercise are funded with time.

## Current recommendation

**Use Plan B as the collaboration default, while maintaining Plan A as the guaranteed minimum publishable study.**

Plan C should be treated as a separate benchmark-development track unless the team deliberately replaces the original project.
