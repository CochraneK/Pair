# License status

No open-source or open-data licence has been selected for PAIR yet.

Public repository visibility is currently used for research transparency and collaboration; it should **not** be interpreted as a blanket permission to reuse, redistribute, train on, or republish all repository content.

Until the research team freezes authorship, benchmark-release strategy, source licences and data-governance decisions:

- code, text and benchmark materials remain subject to the rights of their respective creators/rightsholders;
- third-party sources retain their original licences/copyright;
- do not redistribute source documents whose redistribution permission has not been verified;
- a future software licence and a future dataset/benchmark licence may be different.

A formal licence should be selected before a public benchmark release or archival research release.
